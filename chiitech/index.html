CHIITECH
The AI business assistant & audit companion built for Nigerian SMEs.

The problem

Most small businesses keep sales, expenses and stock in notebooks or WhatsApp chats. The owner knows their cash balance but not their real profit, whether their records would survive a bank or investor's scrutiny, or what to fix first. When it's time to apply for a loan or bring in outside help, there's nothing bankable to show.

What ChiiTech solves

  • Bookkeeping that happens automatically — record a sale or expense once; profit, stock levels and reports update themselves.
  • Audit-grade trust — a tamper-evident audit trail, anomaly detection, and a plain-language audit opinion, without hiring an auditor for day-to-day peace of mind.
  • Tax clarity — VAT and withholding tax calculated from real records instead of guesswork at filing time.
  • A real growth plan — cash-flow forecasts and specific, data-backed suggestions across sales, marketing, finance and operations.
  • Built for teams — a company admin can bring on workers and control exactly what each one can see and do.

Who it's for

Fashion sellers, electronics shops, provisions stores, salons, and small restaurants — any business that wants to look bankable and stay in control as it grows, without hiring a full finance team to get there.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ChiiTech provides business-management, bookkeeping, reporting and audit-support tools. This policy explains how information is handled when an organisation or an invited team member uses the service. It is a working product policy and must be completed with the operator's legal identity and contact details before public launch.

Who is responsible for your data?

The ChiiTech service operator is responsible for deciding why and how personal data is processed. The operator's full registered legal name, business address and designated privacy contact must be published here before public onboarding. A company using ChiiTech remains responsible for the personal data it uploads about its customers, employees and suppliers.

Information we process

  • Account and identity details, including name, email address, Google sign-in identity, role and invitation status.
  • Organisation details and business records entered or imported, such as sales, expenses, orders, stock, customer and supplier details, tax settings, receipts and audit records.
  • Security and service information needed to authenticate accounts, enforce role permissions, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain the service.
  • Questions, selected business figures and staged import information submitted to the AI assistant or AI import-review feature.

Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide company workspaces; process business records; generate dashboards, audit trails, tax calculations and forecasts; analyse staged imports; respond to support requests; protect the service; and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell customer or business records.

Service providers and international processing

ChiiTech uses Supabase for authentication, database and file-storage services. The production database is hosted in the EU West (Ireland) region. Google is used when a person chooses Google sign-in. The AI assistant and import-review feature send the relevant question and the minimum business records needed to answer it to Cloudflare Workers AI. Requests are authenticated and business records are retrieved under the signed-in user's company and role permissions. These providers process information under their own service terms and applicable data-processing arrangements. Data may therefore be processed outside Nigeria. The operator must confirm and document the applicable safeguards and provider retention terms before launch.

Access, sharing and security

Company data is intended to be separated by organisation and role. Workers receive access assigned by their company administrator; auditors receive a restricted read-only view. Platform administration is separately restricted. We use authentication, access controls and managed infrastructure, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should only upload information they are authorised to process and should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information to AI features.

Retention and account closure

We retain records while needed to provide the service, maintain audit history, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Closing an account in the current product deactivates the account or company; it does not necessarily erase all historical records immediately. Backups, security records and legally required records may remain for a limited period. A final retention schedule and deletion-request process must be published before launch.

Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of or portability of their personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. A company administrator may export available company audit information from the product. Individuals may also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Requests should be directed to the operator's published privacy contact once supplied.

Children

ChiiTech is a business service and is not designed for children. Do not create an account for a child or upload children's personal information unless there is a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

Before launch, the operator must add its legal name, address and privacy contact; confirm provider contracts, international-transfer safeguards and retention periods; and obtain review by a Nigerian data-protection lawyer. This draft is not a certification of NDPA compliance.

Cookies and Local Storage

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The current ChiiTech browser client stores its sign-in session and short-lived login-throttling information in browser sessionStorage. These items are scoped to the browser tab/session and are not used as advertising trackers. Business records are loaded from the ChiiTech backend rather than being treated as a permanent local browser database.

Third-party sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, Google may use its own cookies or browser storage on Google's pages to complete authentication. Those technologies are controlled by Google and are subject to Google's privacy and cookie notices.

AI and external services

When you use AI-powered features, relevant questions, selected figures or staged import data are sent to the configured AI provider to generate the requested response. This transfer is described in the Privacy Policy. ChiiTech does not currently describe or rely on advertising-tracking cookies.

Managing storage

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing session storage signs you out locally, but it does not delete server-side company records or close your account. Company data and account closure are handled through the service and applicable data-protection process.

This notice should be rechecked whenever analytics, embedded services, payment checkout or new browser-storage mechanisms are introduced.
