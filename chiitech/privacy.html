Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ChiiTech ("we", "our", "us") is a business-management and audit web application for small businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using ChiiTech you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash — we never see or store your plain password).
  • Business data: the records you or your team enter — sales, expenses, products, customers, orders, tax figures, and receipts you upload.
  • Team data: roles, departments, and invitation details for members you add to your company.
  • Security & audit data: an append-only audit trail of actions taken in the app, used for accountability and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Technical data: browser type, pages visited, and error information, used only to keep the service running and improve it.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the service: your books, dashboards, reports, and exports.
  • To secure your account and detect unusual activity (security alerts).
  • To send transactional messages (invitations, password resets, confirmations).
  • To improve performance and fix bugs.

3. AI features

ChiiTech offers optional AI assistance for analysing imported records and answering questions about your figures. When you use these features, relevant business data is sent to our AI provider to generate a response. Your data is not used to train AI models. AI findings are advisory only — they never make changes to your records.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • We never share your business records with other companies.
  • We never store your password in readable form.
  • We never place secret API keys in the browser — all privileged work happens server-side.

5. Who can see your data

Your data belongs to your company. It is protected by row-level security: a signed-in user can only access their own company's records. Company admins control what workers can see through department permissions. External auditors get read-only access only when you explicitly invite them, and you can revoke that at any time. ChiiTech administrators do not browse your business data; platform-level operations are limited to account management and security.

6. Storage & security

Data is stored on Supabase (Google Cloud infrastructure) and the site is served by Cloudflare. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), row-level database security, append-only audit logging, and staged imports so nothing is written to your books without review.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your company and all associated records at any time by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required by Nigerian law for tax or audit purposes.

8. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or export your data from inside the app (Reports & exports).
  • Delete your data by contacting us.
  • Revoke third-party access (auditor invites) at any time.
  • Opt out of optional AI features by simply not using them.

9. Third-party services

We rely on: Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), and an AI provider (optional AI features only). Each processes data only on our instructions.

10. Children

ChiiTech is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after a change means acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@chiitech.pages.dev
