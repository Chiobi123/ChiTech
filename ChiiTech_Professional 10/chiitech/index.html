CHIITECH
The AI business assistant & audit companion built for Nigerian SMEs.

The problem

Most small businesses keep sales, expenses and stock in notebooks or WhatsApp chats. The owner knows their cash balance but not their real profit, whether their records would survive a bank or investor's scrutiny, or what to fix first. When it's time to apply for a loan or bring in outside help, there's nothing bankable to show.

What ChiiTech solves

  • Bookkeeping that happens automatically — record a sale or expense once; profit, stock levels and reports update themselves.
  • Audit-grade trust — a tamper-evident audit trail, anomaly detection, and a plain-language audit opinion, without hiring an auditor for day-to-day peace of mind.
  • Tax clarity — VAT and withholding tax calculated from real records instead of guesswork at filing time.
  • A real growth plan — cash-flow forecasts and specific, data-backed suggestions across sales, marketing, finance and operations.
  • Built for teams — a company admin can bring on workers and control exactly what each one can see and do.

Who it's for

Fashion sellers, electronics shops, provisions stores, salons, and small restaurants — any business that wants to look bankable and stay in control as it grows, without hiring a full finance team to get there.

Cookie & Data Storage Policy

This build does not use tracking cookies. Your business data — products, sales, expenses, customers, and everything else — is stored server-side in a managed database, not in your browser. Your browser only holds your current sign-in session (cleared automatically when you close the tab), kept in sessionStorage, not a cookie. If ChiiTech adds analytics or third-party embeds later (e.g. Paystack's own checkout may set its own cookies, governed by its own policy), this section will be updated to reflect that.

Deleting your data

Closing your browser tab ends your session but does not delete anything — your data lives on the server, not your device. Use the account-deletion option in Settings to deactivate your account or close your company's account; see that page for exactly what happens and how it can be undone.
